# AMC Controlled Free Tester Package

## 1. Current Test Status

This is a free controlled test of AMC with 2-3 trusted users. Payment is intentionally inactive, and this test is not a broad public launch, paid launch, or commercial conversion test.

The goal is to learn whether AMC helps people understand the structure behind a meaningful career decision. Honest feedback matters more than praise. Testers should use only the public product link:

[https://app.allofmycareer.com/amc-web-mvp](https://app.allofmycareer.com/amc-web-mvp)

AMC is a career decision structure report and structured interpretation tool. It helps users see pressure, risk, safety margin, and decision conditions. It is not a general coaching chatbot, motivational advice tool, admissions consulting service, or a tool that chooses Option A or Option B for the user.

**AMC는 구조를 봅니다. AMC sees structure. Tip in. Decide. Value up.**

## 2. Ideal Tester Profile

An ideal tester:

- Has 10+ years of work experience, preferably.
- Is currently facing a meaningful career decision.
- Is willing to answer 29 questions seriously using real context.
- Is willing to provide candid feedback.
- Is comfortable testing an early-stage product.

Relevant decisions may include:

- Stay vs exit.
- Current company vs independent path.
- MBA, EMBA, or PhD decision.
- Overseas relocation.
- Industry transition.
- Role upgrade or downgrade.
- Burnout-driven decision.
- Family constraint-heavy decision.

This test is not ideal for someone who:

- Wants quick advice.
- Expects AMC to choose for them.
- Is unwilling to provide real context.
- Expects a finished human consulting session.

## 10. Go / No-Go Criteria After 2-3 Testers

### Proceed to the next iteration if

- At least two testers complete the full flow.
- At least two testers say the report helped them see their decision more structurally.
- At least one tester says they would consider paying later.
- No blocking technical issue appears.
- No tester misunderstands AMC as a chatbot-first product.

### Pause and fix if

- Users cannot complete the 29 questions.
- The report is not seen as valuable.
- Executive Report Q&A causes confusion.
- Payment simulation wording causes too much confusion.
- The mobile flow fails.
- English or Korean language feels awkward enough to interfere with testing.
