# V2 integrated decision analysis

The previous V2 route let a customer finish intake and save a polished-looking brief before public research had run. The deterministic engine's explanations were reduced to labels; source cards were separate from the structural reading, and the decision map was a row of intake cards.

The repair makes the final action explicitly **Analyze with public evidence**. It shows the exact public topic, geography and selected research areas before making one request through the existing AMC Perplexity endpoint. A separate action permits an explicitly labelled structural draft. Opening/printing is disabled while research is pending. Research failure remains honest and retryable; it does not manufacture evidence.

`amcV2Analysis.ts` derives one non-scored customer interpretation from the existing engine bands and immutable evidence snapshot. Dashboard, interactive scenario and report share it. The interpretation connects recovery capacity, readiness, support and execution constraints, maps public facts to the five established framework perspectives, and retains source facts, names, URLs and dates. A bounded academic context within the existing general career family connects conferences/research to actual appointment requirements and teaching/research evidence. It does not add a new case family or score customer prose.

The existing ProductApplicationV3, Safety Margin derivation, guardrails, Changing families and scenario engine remain unchanged. Public market or appointment facts do not establish an individual's employability, product demand or recovery capacity. The display adapter links each search-summary claim, source scope, current condition, limited structural interpretation, unresolved personal question and next check without adding a score or changing the core posture. The original evidence snapshot and citations remain intact. Canonical FIFWM statuses now come only from the core's five FIFWM scores; absent scoring inputs stay **not established** rather than borrowing readiness, support or Safety Margin bands.

The relationship diagram connects the protected current base, the target path, public requirements, the actual Changing play and Safety Margin/Switching review. Safety Margin shows its three actual qualitative dimensions and distinguishes unknown from weak. Changing now displays every field of the core play, including what changes, what it protects, evidence, exposure and time exposure; public evidence does not rewrite these plays. Switching reruns the same engine for each selected threshold and shows the actual before/after results and remaining constraints. The simulator offers shared reading, map and plan views for combined assumptions and keeps the baseline and source snapshot intact.

The brief retains 8 structural pages / 9 pages with live or synthetic sources, with substantive reasoning, an actual diagram, source-to-condition interpretation, Changing and a concrete 30/60/90 validation plan. Unchecked/failed research is explicitly a structural draft. Existing allofmycareer filenames and customer-local date behavior are retained, with an academic topic filename added. Unpinned Korean intake switches the interface to Korean; an explicitly selected language is respected.

Privacy remains bounded: the same five public request fields are accepted; the visible public target replaces implicit forwarding of a whole option sentence. Internal bands, notes, custom conditions, analysis prose and source bodies do not enter Founder storage or logs. The V2 API now returns HTTP 503 for unavailable evidence (HTTP 200 only for live evidence), with a categorical `X-AMC-Evidence-Reason` response header distinguishing configuration, provider, source, parse, load and execution failures without exposing provider text. Its existing 30-second provider timeout runs in a 60-second Vercel function window. No new credential, environment change, Production deployment, merge or V2 JEV activation is part of this repair.
