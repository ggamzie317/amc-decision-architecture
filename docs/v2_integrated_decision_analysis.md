# V2 integrated decision analysis

The previous V2 route let a customer finish intake and save a polished-looking brief before public research had run. The deterministic engine's explanations were reduced to labels; source cards were separate from the structural reading, and the decision map was a row of intake cards.

The repair makes the final action explicitly **Analyze with public evidence**. It shows the exact public topic, geography and selected research areas before making one request through the existing AMC Perplexity endpoint. A separate action permits an explicitly labelled structural draft. Opening/printing is disabled while research is pending. Research failure remains honest and retryable; it does not manufacture evidence.

`amcV2Analysis.ts` derives one non-scored customer interpretation from the existing engine bands and immutable evidence snapshot. Dashboard, interactive scenario and report share it. The interpretation connects recovery capacity, readiness, support and execution constraints, maps public facts to the five established framework perspectives, and retains source facts, names, URLs and dates. A bounded academic context within the existing general career family connects conferences/research to actual appointment requirements and teaching/research evidence. It does not add a new case family or score customer prose.

The existing ProductApplicationV3, Safety Margin derivation, guardrails, Changing families and scenario engine remain unchanged. Public market or appointment facts do not establish an individual's employability, product demand or recovery capacity. The adapter now includes actual live facts in the core's outside-evidence narrative, while keeping personal validation unknown. Canonical FIFWM scores remain unavailable when the intake does not supply the canonical scoring inputs. The five customer-facing perspectives are explicitly interpretations, not invented numerical scores.

The relationship diagram connects the protected current base, the target path, public requirements, the actual Changing play and Safety Margin/Switching review. Safety Margin shows its three actual qualitative dimensions and distinguishes unknown from weak. Changing includes moves, prerequisites and evidence to collect. Switching reruns the same engine for each selected threshold and shows the actual before/after results and remaining constraints. The simulator offers shared reading, map and plan views for combined assumptions and keeps the baseline and source snapshot intact.

The brief retains 8 structural pages / 9 pages with live or synthetic sources, with substantive reasoning, an actual diagram, source-to-condition interpretation, Changing and a concrete 30/60/90 validation plan. Unchecked/failed research is explicitly a structural draft. Existing allofmycareer filenames and customer-local date behavior are retained, with an academic topic filename added. Unpinned Korean intake switches the interface to Korean; an explicitly selected language is respected.

Privacy remains bounded: the same five public request fields are accepted; the visible public target replaces implicit forwarding of a whole option sentence. Internal bands, notes, custom conditions, analysis prose and source bodies do not enter Founder storage or logs. No new credential, environment change, Production deployment, merge or V2 JEV activation is part of this repair.

Local verification: 282/282 web tests across 16 files, TypeScript, web/server build, Founder Ops ESM and `git diff --check` pass. Tests include the established 72 paired structural-equivalence fixtures and new assertions for derived interactions, conservative public-evidence integration, scenario changes with remaining constraints, immutable snapshots, unknown dimensions, shared customer surfaces, privacy projection and the academic filename.

Browser/PDF QA uses an explicitly synthetic academic response, not a live provider result. The guided public-research action makes one request with the existing five approved fields. Four visible scenario controls, changed readings, reset, report opening and printing retain the same baseline and source snapshot without another request. Korean 390px/430px dashboard and 390px report have no horizontal overflow; no browser exception occurs. Academic KO, entrepreneurship EN and industry-transition KO briefs each print as nine A4 pages. All nine academic pages were rendered and inspected; named print-page margins prevent edge clipping and extra blank pages. These checks do not establish provider availability or validate synthetic claims.
