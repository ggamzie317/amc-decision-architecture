# V2 self-diagnostic input mapping (Task 060B.1)

This is an input and display map, not a new assessment model. Each case uses the same five card roles with case-specific questions and evidence examples. A card's evidence-status answer is a session-only self-report; it never sets a band, verifies an external fact, or enters the provider request. The customer selects each existing structural band separately. The progress count is completed card inputs, not readiness or probability.

| Customer question/card role | Fact or self-assessment collected | Existing input field | Existing analysis path | Kind | If information is lacking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What public requirement or demand still needs checking in this case? | Chosen research dimension, optional public topic/region, and self-reported check status | `externalAreas`, `publicSearchTarget`, `targetGeography`; separate session-only evidence status | Existing public-evidence request and Missing Point/validation focus; **no** personal external-validation band | Public source only after successful source check; card status is self-report | No source-backed claim; public evidence remains unavailable and the personal requirement remains unverified. “Not checked” is not weak demand. |
| What can you already perform, and what is missing? | Explicit readiness band and missing resource choices; separately reported basis | `bands.internalReadiness`, `missingAssets` | `buildCurrentCaseStructuralSignals` selection 17 → existing posture/Changing | Self-report | Explicit `unknown` remains unknown; no strength inferred from a check or confidence. |
| What spending/loss can you carry? | **Two separate** explicit answers for financial room and downside exposure | `bands.financialRoom`, `bands.downsideExposure` | Existing selections 19 and 21 → Safety Margin and guardrails | Self-report | Each unanswered field remains unanswered; explicit unknown is retained as unknown. |
| What stopping/re-entry route exists? | Explicit reversibility answer and basis | `bands.reversibility` | Existing selection 20 → Safety Margin and guardrails | Self-report | Unknown route remains unknown; no inferred recovery capacity. |
| What time/constraints and support are actually available? | **Two separate** explicit answers for constraint load and Option B support, with source choices | `bands.constraintLoad`, `bands.optionBSupport`, `constraints`, `supportSources` | Existing selections 25 and 23 → posture/Changing | Self-report | Unknown support/load stays unknown. A checked item cannot set either band. |

The V2 step numbers above are not canonical question numbers. The canonical current-case adapter uses selections 17, 19, 20, 21, 23, and 25 by **field meaning**, independently of the V2 step index. The canonical report's FIFWM factor scores come from distinct `Q18_score`–`Q22_score` inputs in `src/report_payload_builder.py`; the V2 cards do not collect those scored factor inputs. `buildV2Input` must therefore keep FIFWM unavailable instead of substituting finance, readiness, support or card status. The missing canonical factor inputs are Formal, Informal, Framework, Workflow and Market/Policy scored evidence.

The five roles are adapted to each of the nine existing case types. Their examples are prompts for what a person might verify, not new thresholds or proof of verification. The six structural bands, their original labels and the core analysis functions stay unchanged.
